### 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am a senior-level biomedical researcher, medical educator, and healthcare data strategist with a **PhD in Neuroscience** from the **Medical University of South Carolina**. My expertise spans synaptic electrophysiology, doctoral curriculum design, and public health epidemiology.

As a former **Healthcare Data Analyst & Interim Program Manager** for the **Florida Department of Health (Duval County - CDC OD2A Program)**, I directed county-level EMS, emergency department syndromic surveillance, and prescription drug monitoring program (PDMP) analytics. I specialize in translating complex biological, epidemiological, and socioeconomic datasets into actionable, decision-grade intelligence pipelines, interactive dashboards, and rigorous econometric models.

### 🚀 Featured Data Intelligence Projects

#### 1. [Opioid Surveillance & Primary Care Capacity Analysis](https://github.com/jpenabravoj00/opioid-surveillance-catchment-analysis)
> **Mapping High-Risk Overdose Hotspots to Safety-Net Clinic Catchment Areas**
* **Executive Summary**: Analyzed official open-access **CDC VSRR** mortality surveillance and **HRSA** Health Center Program directories to identify geographic regions where synthetic fentanyl surges (`T40.4`) and methamphetamine polysubstance co-use (`T43.6`) mathematically saturate Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) primary care capacity.
* **Key Innovations**: Engineered three custom decision metrics—*Clinic Catchment Misalignment Index*, *Synthetic Surge Velocity*, and *Polysubstance Cross-Over Index*—backed by weighted DAX measures and December calendar-year suppression controls.
* **Tech Stack & Artifacts**: R (Tidyverse 4.4), Power BI Service, DAX, CDC VSRR, HRSA Open Data.
* 🔗 **Live Interactive Dashboard**: [Power BI Web Report](https://app.powerbi.com/reportEmbed?reportId=05ad9cac-2c0a-4517-826a-0dce97e81d46&autoAuth=true&ctid=c82f2d55-67d0-4a4a-8820-2f84a18c1cdd) | 📂 [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/jpenabravoj00/opioid-surveillance-catchment-analysis)

#### 2. [National Childcare Affordability & Disparity Analysis (NDCP)](https://github.com/jpenabravoj00/ndcp-analysis)
> **Econometric Surveillance & Geospatial Disparity Modeling (2008–2022)**
* **Executive Summary**: Analyzed the US Department of Labor Women's Bureau National Database of Childcare Prices (NDCP) covering county-level costs across all 50 states over 14 years. Modeled longitudinal price growth trajectories (+79.7% for infant center care), state-level disparity benchmarks, and income elasticity correlations ($r = 0.58$).
* **Interactive Artifact**: Standalone interactive HTML report with Plotly charts for interactive county-level filtering, hover tooltips, and elasticity modeling.
* **Tech Stack & Artifacts**: Python 3.10+, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, OpenPyXL, Jupytext.
* 📊 **Interactive Report**: [HTML Report Preview]((https://jpenabravoj00.github.io/ndcp-analysis/)) | 📂 [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/jpenabravoj00/ndcp-analysis)
